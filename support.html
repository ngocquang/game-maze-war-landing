Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Hold anywhere on the screen to fire soldiers from your Summon Gate. Drag left or right to choose an entrance of the maze. Send your army through multiplier gates and break the red Maze Core before time runs out.

What are Rune Doors?

A Rune Door opens one branch at a time and switches on its own timer. It flashes 1.5 seconds before it switches, and its arrow always shows the open branch. Stop firing before a door leads your soldiers into a dead end.

My Summon Gate stops firing.

The Gate overheats when you fire for too long. Release your finger to cool it down, then hold again.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Settings and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple ID or Google account that you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple or Google. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone) or in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions (Android).

Will my progress move to a new phone?

No. Progress is saved only on your device, and the game has no account or cloud save in this version. Your purchase can be restored on a new device.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings and tap "Reset progress", then confirm. Your settings are kept.

Which languages does the game support?

English and Vietnamese. The game follows your device language, and you can change it in Settings.
