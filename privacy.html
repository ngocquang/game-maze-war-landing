Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

In short: Nuna Games does not run any server and does not receive your personal data. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to serve them.

Who we are

Maze War ("the game") is published by Nuna Games ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the game on iOS and Android and to this website.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data ourselves. The game has no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no server of ours. We do not receive the data that the advertising service below collects.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show rewarded ads. You choose to watch an ad in exchange for a reward, such as double gold or a revive. The ads come from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK collects data from your device and sends it to Google. This data can include:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (Android) or the IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking).
  • Your IP address and the approximate location that comes from it.
  • Your interactions with ads, such as views and taps.
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance data of the SDK.

Google handles this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before it loads ads. You can change your choice later with the "Privacy options" button in Settings.
  • On iOS, the game asks for permission to track. If you do not allow it, the IDFA is not shared, and ads are not personalized with it.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings.
  • Watching ads is optional. The in-app purchase gives the same rewards without ads.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, settings and purchase status in a file on your device. This file never leaves your device. If you delete the game, the file is deleted. You can also clear your progress from the game's Settings screen.

In-app purchases

The game offers one optional purchase. Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) processes the payment. We do not receive your name, email, card number or any other payment details. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to the payment.

Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location. On iOS it asks for permission to track, as described above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ad requests use a maximum ad content rating of Teen.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or trackers. GitHub may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how data is collected, for example with online play, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.
