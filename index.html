Why players love it

Not a straight lane. A maze.

Rune Doors switch paths on their own timer. You win by choosing the right entrance at the right moment, not only by firing more.

Crush the Core

Pour a stream of blue soldiers into the maze and break the enemy Maze Core across three waves.

Time the Rune Doors

Each door opens one branch at a time and flashes before it switches. Stop firing before a dead end.

Multiply your army

Hit ×2, ×3 and ×4 gates to grow your army. Dodge the minus and divide traps on the long routes.

Unleash the Ultimate

Collapse a corridor on the red horde, or call the Wall-Breaker Golem to smash through walls.

10 handcrafted mazes

Every level adds a new rule: Pits, breakable walls, bonus boxes and faction doors.

Earn 3 stars

Win fast and keep your Rampart strong. Spend gold to upgrade your Summon Gate and soldiers.

Easy to learn

Hold. Drag. Win.

One hand, portrait mode, one or two minutes a match.

Hold to shoot

Hold anywhere on the screen. Your Summon Gate fires a steady stream of soldiers.

Drag to aim

Slide the Gate left or right to choose which entrance of the maze your army takes.

Read the doors

Watch the arrows. Release before a door leads into a Pit, and bait the red army into it.
