Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

In short: Maze War does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Maze War ("the game") is published by Nuna Games ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the game on iOS and Android and to this website.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The game has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no server. It does not use the internet to send data to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, settings and purchase status in a file on your device. This file never leaves your device. If you delete the game, the file is deleted. You can also clear your progress from the game's Settings screen.

In-app purchases

The game offers one optional purchase. Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) processes the payment. We do not receive your name, email, card number or any other payment details. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to the payment.

Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, no data about children is stored by us.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or trackers. GitHub may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that collect data, such as ads or online play, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.
